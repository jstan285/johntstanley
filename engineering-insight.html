Engineering insight

Engineering insight that informs investment

Different teams can describe the same initiative and reveal very different needs. I investigate how those accounts fit together, so you can choose where to invest and turn the findings into work to carry out.

Let’s explore your challenge

When this can help

  • A decision is stalled by different views of the problem
  • You need to know where a technology investment will help
  • Findings need to become a practical next step
01

Discovery

Find the question beneath the request

What this can produce

A shared view of the problem

  • An investigation plan and key questions
  • The work, constraints, and information gaps

A project request may begin with a recurring delivery problem or competing views of what needs to change. I shape the investigation around your decision and the people closest to the work. We distinguish observations, established findings, and questions that need further investigation.

02

Direction

Make the choices and their implications clear

What this can produce

A basis for choosing where to invest

  • Options and recommendations
  • Tradeoffs, uncertainties, and what to test

I investigate options and work through the capability, information, working practices, and dependencies each requires. Recommendations make the technical implications understandable, explain why a direction fits, and identify what should be tested before committing further.

03

Implementation

Carry the findings into the next piece of work

What this can produce

A practical order of work

  • Requirements and related initiatives
  • Dependencies and responsibilities

An investigation can reveal changes that depend on one another. I translate findings into requirements and sequenced initiatives, then can stay involved to coordinate contributors and adjust the plan as implementation reveals more about the work.

Working together

A clear role in the work.

I can lead a focused investigation, help shape a technical program, or coordinate related initiatives and follow-on work. We agree on the decision to support, the people involved, and how to review progress.
